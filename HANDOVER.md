# On-call Handover Checklist

## Services
- [ ] Capture service inventory (issue #2): all internal tools, owners, runbooks, dashboards, deployment targets
- [ ] Confirm each service has an up-to-date runbook link

## Escalation
- [ ] Document escalation paths (issue #3): severity levels, working-hours and after-hours contacts, paging policy
- [ ] Verify contact details for primary and secondary escalation contacts

## Incidents
- [ ] Record open incidents (issue #4): status, owners, next actions, deadlines
- [ ] Hand off any pending follow-ups to the incoming on-call

## Handover call
- [ ] Walk through the audit trail in this repository
- [ ] Confirm the incoming on-call can reach all dashboards, repos, and paging tools
